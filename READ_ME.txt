This only works with a local hosted server for this local asset
Follow two steps:
1. open Command Prompt in the file's path, and type in:
cd "~:/path/to/file"
python -m http.server 8000

2. open chrome and go to:
http://localhost:8000

The scene might not appear immediately, try turning the immersive Web Emulator on and off until the heatset icon appears and click to enter.

On your left controller, 
thumb stick controls the movement of the most recently spawned model (the model)
x resets the model

On your right controller,
thumb stick controls the yaw of the model, 
A scales up the model,
B scales down the model.
